What is a prediction market?

A market where people trade on questions about the future, like “Will Bitcoin be above $100,000 on Friday?”. Every question has two sides, Yes and No. Each side has a price between 1¢ and 99¢, and that price is what the crowd thinks the odds are: 20¢ means about a 20% chance. When the answer is known, every winning share pays $1 and every losing share pays nothing.

What is Polymarket?

One of the world's largest prediction markets. MOONX is an easier way to use it: your trades go to Polymarket, and your winnings come back to your MOONX wallet.

Is this gambling?

No. You buy a share in a real market, and you can sell it back at any time before the result. A share bought at 4¢ is still worth about 4¢ right after you buy it, not zero. The price is set by everyone trading, not by a house, and MOONX never bets against you.

Where does my money go?

Into your own wallet, and it stays there. MOONX never holds it. When you trade, the order goes to Polymarket. When the market ends, your winnings come back to the same wallet.

What does “your money stays in your wallet” mean?

Only you can move your money. Your wallet is created on your phone when you sign up. You keep one backup file and a password that only you know. With them, you can take your money out at any moment, without asking us.

Do I need crypto to start?

No. Add money with a card, like any online purchase. If you already have crypto (USDC), you can send it from another wallet or exchange. Inside MOONX, you never pay network fees: we cover them on every trade, sale and payout.

Do I have to wait for the result?

No. You can sell anytime before the end, at the current price. Like any trade, a sale needs a buyer on the other side: instant on busy markets, slower on quiet ones, where you may have to lower your price. If your share went from 5¢ to 30¢, you can take 6x now instead of waiting. If it's going the wrong way, you can sell and keep what's left.

What can I lose?

Only what you put in, nothing more. $10 on a trade means $10 at risk. No leverage, no margin calls, no debt.

Why do some payouts look so big?

Because the price shows the odds. A share at 5¢ pays $1 if you're right, which is 20x. A share at 90¢ also pays $1, which is 1.1x. Big wins come from unlikely outcomes, and most unlikely outcomes don't happen. Only put in what you can afford to lose.

How does MOONX make money?

A 1% fee on each trade. If you invite a friend, you earn up to 0.5% each time they buy or sell, paid from our share, not theirs.
