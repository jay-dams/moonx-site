What is a Polymarket builder?

Not everyone who trades on Polymarket does it on polymarket.com. Many use apps built on top of it, and Polymarket has a program for them.

By MOONX Team·Updated 27 October 2026·3 min read
Short answer

A Polymarket builder is a person, team or company that routes orders from users to Polymarket through its own app. Each order carries the builder's code, which is recorded on-chain, so matched trades count toward the builder's volume. A builder's volume can appear on the public leaderboard at builders.polymarket.com, which ranks the top builders. Builders get gas-free on-chain operations through Polymarket's relayer. MOONX is a Polymarket builder.

The definition

Polymarket's documentation describes a builder as one that "routes orders from users to Polymarket". In practice, that means an app with its own design and features, whose trades all happen on Polymarket's order book.

The Builders Program gives these apps three things:

  • A builder code. It is attached to every order and written on-chain as part of the signed order. Each matched trade that carries it counts toward the builder's volume.
  • Leaderboard visibility. A builder's volume can appear on the public leaderboard at builders.polymarket.com, which ranks the top builders. It can take up to 24 hours to show.
  • Gas-free operations. Wallet setup, approvals, orders and position moves go through Polymarket's relayer at no gas cost.

Builder levels

Polymarket lists three tiers, mostly about how much traffic an app can send:

TierRelayer transactions per dayHow you get it
Unverified100Create a builder profile
Verified10,000Apply and be approved by Polymarket
PartnerUnlimitedEnterprise level

Verified builders can also be considered for weekly USDC rewards and grants, subject to approval.

What it means when you use a builder app

  • Same markets, same order book. Your order meets other traders on Polymarket, at Polymarket's prices. The market rules and the way results are decided (what is UMA?) are the same.
  • Polymarket's fees still apply. The app may add its own fee on top. Check it before you trade.
  • Your money depends on the app's wallet design. The builder label is about routing and attribution. Who controls your funds is set by each app's own wallet and terms, so read them.
Worked example

You buy $20 of Yes at 50¢ in a sports market on MOONX.

  • You get 20 ÷ 0.50 = 40 shares (before fees).
  • The order goes to Polymarket's order book with MOONX's builder code.
  • Polymarket's taker fee: 40 × 0.05 × 0.50 × 0.50 = $0.50. MOONX's fee, 1% of $20: $0.20. Every fee is included in what you see before you confirm.
  • Once matched, the trade counts toward MOONX's builder volume. Your shares sit in your own wallet.

MOONX as a builder

MOONX is a Polymarket builder. Every trade you make on MOONX is a real Polymarket trade. MOONX's wallet is non-custodial: it is created on your device, and MOONX can't move, freeze or recover your money (what is a non-custodial wallet?). MOONX pays the network fees.
