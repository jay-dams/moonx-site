Earn with MOONX: your crew and trades taken from your posts

MOONX shares its trading fee with the people who bring traders in. There are two ways to earn, they add up, and none of it costs your friends a cent more. Here is how each one works, with the numbers.

By MOONX Team·Updated 27 October 2026·3 min read
Short answer

You earn in two ways on MOONX. Your crew: everyone who joins with your invite link earns you 25% of MOONX's fee on all their trades, for life. Your posts: when someone trades a market from one of your trades, on either side, you earn 25% of MOONX's fee on that trade and their later trades on that market. Both can add up to 50%. It's paid in USDC around the 1st of each month.

Where the money comes from

MOONX earns a fee of 1% of the trade value when someone buys or sells. Part of that fee goes to you. It comes out of MOONX's share, never out of the trader's: your friends pay exactly the same fees as anyone else.

You earn on trading, not on results. You earn the same whether the other person wins or loses.

1. Your crew: 25% for life

Share your invite link. Everyone who opens their account through it joins your crew. You then earn 25% of MOONX's fee on all their trades, buys and sells, for as long as their account runs. No cap.

Worked example

A friend in your crew trades $1,000 in a month. MOONX's fee is 1%, so $10. You earn $2.50. Ten friends who each trade $1,000 a month: $25 a month.

2. Your posts: 25% when people trade from you

Your trades can appear in the MOONX feed, on your profile, or on a card you share. When another user trades a market from one of them, you earn 25% of MOONX's fee on that trade. It works whichever side they take, the same as you or the opposite.

It doesn't stop there: you also earn on their later trades on that market, until it settles, or until they trade that market from someone else's post.

  • Only opening your post earns nothing. They have to trade from it.
  • It doesn't matter whether they had already traded that market before.
  • Your own trades never earn you this reward.

Both add up

If someone in your crew trades from your post, you earn both: 50% of MOONX's fee on that trade. If your crew member trades from someone else's post, you keep your 25% and the author of the post earns theirs.

When you get paid

Earnings build up over each calendar month and are paid together in USDC, into your main wallet, on or around the 1st of the following month. A month under $5 in total isn't paid out: it rolls into the next month.

The rules that keep it fair

Fake accounts, referring yourself, trading from your own posts through another account, groups of accounts trading from each other's posts, and bought or incentivised traffic earn nothing, and the earnings can be reversed. When you share, don't promise returns, don't present MOONX as an investment, and don't send it to people in countries where MOONX isn't available. Earnings are yours once they are paid, MOONX can change, pause or end the programme with notice, and taxes on what you earn are your responsibility. The full rules are in sections 10 and 12 of the Terms.

Common questions

How much do I earn when a friend joins MOONX?

25% of MOONX's fee on every trade they make, buys and sells, for as long as their account runs. MOONX's fee is 1% of the trade value, so that's 0.25% of what they trade.

Does my friend pay more because I invited them?

No. Your earnings come out of MOONX's share of the fee. Your friend pays exactly the same fees as any other user.

Do I earn if someone takes the opposite side of my trade?

Yes. You earn the same reward whichever side they choose.

When are referral earnings paid?

Monthly, in USDC, into your main wallet, around the 1st of the following month. Months under $5 roll over.
