How Polymarket works

Polymarket glossary: every word you will see, explained

Ask, bid, spread, taker, negative risk, UMA. The vocabulary is the steepest part of prediction markets. Here is each word in one or two sentences, in alphabetical order.

By MOONX Team·Updated 27 October 2026·3 min read
Short answer

The words that matter most: a share pays $1 if its outcome is right. The price is between 0 and $1 and reads as a probability. The bid is the best price buyers offer, the ask is the best price sellers accept, and the spread is the gap. A taker fills against existing orders and pays Polymarket's fee. Resolution is when the result is confirmed, usually through the UMA oracle.

A to M

TermMeaning
AskThe lowest price a seller currently accepts. Buying right away fills at the ask.
BidThe highest price a buyer currently offers. Selling right away fills at the bid.
BondMoney a proposer or disputer puts up during resolution, typically $750. They lose it if they're wrong.
BuilderAn app that sends its users' orders to Polymarket and is credited for that volume. MOONX is one.
ChainlinkThe price feed used to settle short crypto Up or Down markets automatically.
ComboSeveral outcomes combined into one position, priced by market makers on request.
DisputeA challenge to a proposed result, made by posting a bond within the challenge window.
DVMUMA's Data Verification Mechanism: the vote by UMA token holders that settles a twice-disputed result.
EjectMOONX's sell button. One tap sells your position at the best price on the book.
Implied probabilityThe chance a price represents. 30¢ means about 30%.
Limit orderAn order with a price limit. It can wait in the book until someone takes it.
LiquidityHow much is available to buy and sell near the current price. More liquidity means smaller price moves when you trade.
MakerA trader whose order waits in the book and is filled by someone else. Makers pay no fee on Polymarket.
Market orderAn order that fills right away at the best available prices.
MidpointHalfway between the best bid and best ask. Polymarket shows it as the price when the spread is 10¢ or less.
MultipleWhat a share pays relative to its price if it wins: 1 divided by the price. 20¢ is 5x.

No sharePays $1 if the outcome does not happen.

OutcomeOne possible answer to a market's question.
PolygonThe blockchain Polymarket runs on. MOONX wallets live on it too.

pUSDThe dollar token Polymarket uses to settle trades, backed by USDC.

Common questions

What is the spread on Polymarket?

The difference between the best price buyers offer (bid) and the best price sellers accept (ask). On busy markets it is often a single tick.

What is the difference between a maker and a taker?

A maker's order waits in the book. A taker's order fills right away against it. On Polymarket, takers pay a fee on most markets and makers pay none.

What does resolution mean?

The point where a market's result is confirmed. Winning shares then pay $1 and losing shares $0.
