How do you make money on Polymarket?

Every profit on Polymarket comes from one of two moves. Both are simple to describe, and neither is easy to repeat.

By MOONX Team·Updated 27 October 2026·3 min read
Short answer

There are two ways. Buy a share and hold it, and if your outcome wins, each share pays $1. Or sell it to another trader before the end for more than you paid, for example buying at 25¢ and selling at 50¢. Either way you profit only if you judged the chance better than the market did, and you can lose everything you put into a trade, plus fees.

Way 1: be right at the end

Buy shares in an outcome and hold them until the market resolves. If it happens, each share pays $1. If not, they pay $0.

Worked example

You put $10 on Yes at 25¢ on a sports market through MOONX: 10 ÷ 0.25 = 40 shares.

  • Polymarket fee: 40 × 0.05 × 0.25 × 0.75 = $0.375
  • MOONX fee: 1% of $10 = $0.10
  • Total cost: $10.475

Yes wins: you receive 40 × $1 = $40, a profit of about $29.53. Yes loses: you lose $10.475.

Way 2: sell higher before the end

You don't need to wait for the result. If the price rises after you buy, you can sell to another trader and keep the difference, even if the outcome never happens in the end.

Worked example

Same 40 shares bought at 25¢ for $10.475 in all. News pushes Yes to 50¢ and you sell.

  • Sale: 40 × 0.50 = $20
  • Polymarket fee: 40 × 0.05 × 0.50 × 0.50 = $0.50
  • MOONX fee: 1% of $20 = $0.20

You keep $19.30, a profit of about $8.83, locked in whatever happens next.

The same works in reverse: if the price falls, selling early limits the loss. More in selling before the result.

The hard part

Both ways need the same thing: the price has to be wrong, and you have to be on the right side of the error. A share at 25¢ already means traders put the chance near 25%. If that's correct, buying it many times roughly breaks even before fees, and fees then push you below zero. You make money over time only if your estimates are better than the market's, which is hard. See expected value.

Cheap shares are a special trap. Big multiples come from unlikely outcomes, and most unlikely outcomes don't happen. A 2026 working paper on 588 million Polymarket trades found that, in aggregate, purchases below 10¢ lost 19.3¢ per dollar. The authors also show that the size of that loss depends on how trades are counted.

Before you start

There is no reliable way to profit on prediction markets. Put in only what you can afford to lose entirely.

Other ways people earn

Polymarket pays daily rewards to traders whose limit orders wait on the book close to the market price, on markets that have a reward set up, see Polymarket rewards. MOONX shares part of its own fee with people who bring in traders, see earn with MOONX. Neither needs you to predict anything, though resting orders carry their own risk.
