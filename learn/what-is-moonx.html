What is MOONX? The social app to trade on Polymarket

MOONX is an app that puts every Polymarket market in a social feed. You see what other traders buy, you take the same side or the other one, and your money stays in a wallet only you can move.

By MOONX Team·Updated 27 October 2026·3 min read
Short answer

MOONX (moonx.fi) is a consumer app built on Polymarket, one of the largest prediction markets. You sign up with an email, add money by card or with USDC, and buy Yes or No on sports, crypto, politics and the news in a few taps. A feed shows real trades from other users. The wallet is non-custodial, MOONX pays the network fees, and it charges 1% per trade.

What you can do on MOONX

  • Trade every Polymarket market. Sports, crypto, politics, the news, culture. Each question has a Yes and a No, priced between 0 and $1, and each winning share pays $1.
  • Follow the feed. Scroll a feed of real trades from other MOONX users. Open one and take the same side, or the other one.
  • Sell whenever you want. The Eject button sells your position before the end, at the best price on the book. See selling before the result.
  • Earn from your trades and your friends. When people trade from your posts, or friends join with your link, you get part of MOONX's fee. See earn with MOONX.

How MOONX differs from using Polymarket directly

The markets, prices and results are Polymarket's. MOONX is a Polymarket builder: an app that sends your orders to Polymarket. What changes is everything around the trade.

What it costs

MOONX charges 1% of the trade value when you buy and when you sell. Nothing when a market resolves, no subscription, no account fee, no withdrawal fee. Polymarket's own fee applies on most markets, depending on the category and price, and every fee is included in what you see before you confirm. Details in Polymarket fees.

Worked example

You put $10 on Yes at 25¢ in a politics market: 40 shares. MOONX's fee is $0.10. Polymarket's fee is 40 × 0.04 × 0.25 × 0.75 = $0.30. If Yes wins, the 40 shares pay $40.

Your money stays yours

MOONX never holds your funds or your keys. Your wallet is created on your device, you keep one encrypted backup file and its password, and a PIN approves every move. MOONX can't move, freeze or recover your money, which is also why the backup file and its password matter so much. More in your MOONX wallet.

Who runs MOONX

MOONX is operated from Hong Kong SAR, as stated in its Terms. It's a Polymarket builder, and it's listed on the Polymarket Tools Directory. You can reach the team at hello@moonx.fi or through @moonxfi on X.

Who can use it

You must be 18, or the age of majority where you live if that's higher. MOONX is not available everywhere: not in the US, the UK, mainland China or Hong Kong, nor anywhere Polymarket restricts access. The full list is in the Terms.

Before you start

Trading on prediction markets is risky. Big multiples come from unlikely outcomes, and most unlikely outcomes don't happen. Only put in what you can afford to lose.

Common questions

Is MOONX the same as Polymarket?

No. Polymarket runs the markets. MOONX is an app built on top of it: your trades go to Polymarket, and your winnings come back to your MOONX wallet.

Is MOONX free?

Signing up is free. MOONX charges 1% of the trade value when you buy or sell, and pays the network fees for you.

Does MOONX have an app?

MOONX works in a browser on your phone or computer: go to moonx.fi, and the app itself is at lfg.moonx.fi. It also runs inside Telegram as a Mini App, at t.me/moonx_app_bot.

How do I start?

Sign up with your email at moonx.fi and explore the app, then set up your wallet and add money. The steps are in your first trade on MOONX.
