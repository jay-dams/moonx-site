What are Polymarket rewards and rebates?

Polymarket pays some traders back. The first two pay traders whose orders wait in the book, which most beginners don't do. Here is what each program says, from Polymarket's own documentation.

By MOONX Team·Updated 27 October 2026·3 min read
Short answer

Polymarket has three programs that pay traders back. Liquidity rewards pay traders whose limit orders wait in the book close to the midpoint, scored once a minute and paid daily. Maker rebates return part of the taker fees each day to makers whose orders get filled, from 15% of fees in sports to 25% in politics. The Taker Rebate Program, started on 28 May 2026, gives traders who pay fees a rebate of 3% to 50% depending on 30-day weighted volume. All three pay daily, with nothing paid below $1.

First, makers and takers

An order that waits in the book is a maker order (what is a limit order?). An order that fills right away against it is a taker order. On most markets takers pay a fee and makers pay none (Polymarket fees). The three programs that pay traders back are built around that split. Polymarket also runs a Referral Program and the Builders Program for apps built on it, which this page doesn't cover.

1. Liquidity rewards (makers)

Paid to traders who keep limit orders resting in the book.

  • Each eligible market sets its own maximum spread from the midpoint, minimum order size and reward budget, shown in the market's details.
  • Orders closer to the midpoint score more. Scores fall to zero at the maximum spread.
  • Quoting on both sides scores best. One side alone still earns, at a third of the rate. If the midpoint is under 10¢ or over 90¢, only two-sided quotes count.
  • The book is sampled once a minute, so up to 24 × 60 = 1,440 samples a day. Your share of the day's score is your share of that market's rewards.
  • Paid daily at midnight UTC. Amounts under $1 are not paid out.

2. Maker rebates (makers)

Taker fees fund a daily rebate for makers whose orders get filled. The share returned depends on the category:

CategoryShare of taker fees returned
Crypto20%
Sports15%
Finance, politics, economics, culture, weather, tech, mentions, other25%
GeopoliticsNo fee, no rebate

Your part is your filled maker volume, weighted by the same price curve as the fee, compared with all other makers in that market. Paid daily in pUSD, once you reach $1.

3. Taker rebates (takers)

Started on 28 May 2026. Every fee-paying trade earns weighted volume:

wV = trade size × (1 − price) × category weight × any bonus

Weights: sports 1.0; politics, finance, mentions, tech 1.3; economics, culture, weather, other 1.7; crypto 2.3; geopolitics 0. Your 30-day total sets your tier:

Tier30-day weighted volumeRebateOne-time bonus
Bronze$2,0003%$10
Silver$20,0008%$50
Gold$200,00018%$250
Platinum$1,000,00032%$1,500
Diamond$4,000,00044%$7,500
Obsidian$10,000,00050%$25,000

The rebate applies only to trades made after you reach a tier, with no backfill. Paid daily at midnight UTC in pUSD, from $1. Polymarket's docs add that third-party integrations using omnibus wallets are not eligible.

Worked example

You trade sports markets at 50¢. Each dollar traded earns 1 × (1 − 0.50) × 1.0 = $0.50 of weighted volume.

To reach Bronze, you need $2,000 of weighted volume in 30 days: $2,000 ÷ 0.50 = $4,000 of trades. Polymarket's own example: 1,000 politics shares at 40¢ is a $400 trade, worth 400 × 0.60 × 1.3 = $312 of weighted volume.

Read the fine print

Polymarket can change rates, weights, thresholds and bonuses at any time, and can remove rebates for wash trading or self-matching. None of these programs makes a losing trade a winning one. Check the current pages before counting on a number.
