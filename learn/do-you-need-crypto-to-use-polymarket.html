Do you need crypto to use Polymarket?

Polymarket runs on a blockchain, which makes many people think they need bitcoin or a crypto exchange account. The real requirement is narrower. Your money has to be in Polymarket's dollar token.

By MOONX Team·Updated 27 October 2026·3 min read
Short answer

To trade on Polymarket, your money has to be in pUSD, Polymarket's dollar token on the Polygon blockchain, backed by USDC. You don't need to own bitcoin or any coin whose price moves, because pUSD is a dollar token. Polymarket's own deposit guides describe sending crypto from another wallet, but apps built on Polymarket, such as MOONX, let you pay by card, mobile wallet or bank transfer and convert the money for you.

What Polymarket actually requires

Every trade on Polymarket is paid for in pUSD, a token on the Polygon blockchain backed by USDC, a dollar stablecoin (what is pUSD?). A winning share pays 1 pUSD. So, strictly speaking, yes: the money you trade with is a crypto token.

But it is a dollar token, not a coin with a moving price. Polymarket's documentation says pUSD's backing is enforced by a smart contract, with no algorithmic peg and no fractional reserve. You are betting on events, not on the price of a cryptocurrency, unless you choose a crypto market.

Two ways to get there

Send crypto you already have. Polymarket's help center and developer docs describe deposits as crypto transfers: you get a deposit address and send tokens from a supported network (Ethereum and other EVM chains, Solana, Bitcoin, Tron). They are converted to pUSD automatically. This route assumes you already hold crypto somewhere and know which network to use.

Pay with ordinary money through an app. Some apps built on Polymarket handle the conversion. On MOONX, you can add money by card, mobile wallet or bank transfer through payment partners (Stripe and Swapped, via Calm Treasury). The partner's fee is shown before you pay, and payment partners may run their own identity checks. The money arrives as USDC in your MOONX wallet and moves to Polymarket as pUSD when you trade.

Worked example

You have never owned crypto. You want to put $30 on a market.

  1. In MOONX, choose to add money and pay $30 by card.
  2. The partner's screen shows its fee and how much USDC you will receive. You confirm.
  3. The USDC lands in your MOONX wallet on Polygon.
  4. You buy Yes at 60¢ with $20. Before fees, that is 20 ÷ 0.60 ≈ 33.3 shares. MOONX moves the $20 to Polymarket as pUSD for you.

At no point did you open a crypto exchange account, pick a network or buy a gas token. MOONX pays the network fees.

What you still take on

Not needing to buy crypto doesn't make the money less on-chain. A few things stay true:

  • Your wallet is yours to keep. MOONX is non-custodial: you hold one backup file and its password. Lose either one and the money is gone for good (what is a non-custodial wallet?).
  • Withdrawals are blockchain transfers. On MOONX they go only to addresses on your Payout Wallets list, and a transfer to a wrong address can't be reversed.
  • Availability depends on where you live. Polymarket restricts some countries, and MOONX is not available everywhere (including the US, the UK, mainland China and Hong Kong; see the Terms). Check the rules where you live.
