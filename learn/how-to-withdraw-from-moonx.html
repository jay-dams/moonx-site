How do you withdraw money from MOONX?

Your money is in your own wallet, so only you can send it out. It needs one setup step first, and one careful check every time.

By MOONX Team·Updated 27 October 2026·3 min read
Short answer

Add the address you want to send to, such as an exchange account or another wallet that accepts USDC on Polygon, to your Payout Wallets list, which holds up to five addresses. Then withdraw from the app and confirm with your PIN. MOONX charges no withdrawal fee and pays the network fee. A withdrawal to a wrong address can't be reversed, so check it twice.

Step by step

  1. Make sure the money is free to send. You withdraw money that's available in your wallet, not money that's still in a trade or in savings. Money in a trade comes back when you sell it with Eject or when the market resolves. Money in savings has to come back from Aave first, which depends on Aave having money available.
  2. Find a receiving address that accepts USDC on Polygon. On an exchange, that means choosing USDC and the Polygon network when you look up your deposit address.
  3. Add it to your Payout Wallets list. Your wallet can only send withdrawals to addresses on this list, up to five. It's a safeguard that runs in your wallet, not a service MOONX operates.
  4. Withdraw. Choose the address and the amount, then confirm with your PIN. Nothing leaves your wallet unless you choose and confirm it, and once it's sent, MOONX can't stop or reverse it.

What it costs

MOONX charges no withdrawal fee, and it pays the network (gas) fee on the transfer, so you don't need any other token in your wallet. Polymarket charges nothing to withdraw either. The exchange or wallet that receives your USDC may charge its own fee, for example to convert it into your currency. Check its fees before you send.

Worked example

A market resolves your way and your 80 shares pay 80 × $1 = $80 into your main wallet. You already had $15 there, so you hold 80 + 15 = $95. You withdraw all of it to your exchange's USDC on Polygon address, already on your Payout Wallets list. MOONX takes $0 and pays the network fee. Your exchange receives 95 USDC, minus any fee the exchange itself charges.

Check the address, every time

A withdrawal sent to a wrong or compromised address can't be reversed, by MOONX or anyone else. Before you add an address to your Payout Wallets list, check it character by character, and check that it accepts USDC on Polygon. Sending to an address that doesn't can mean losing the money.

Leaving MOONX completely

You don't need MOONX to reach your money. Your wallet is non-custodial, and MOONX's Terms (section 20) state that your funds remain reachable with your Backup through any compatible wallet software. More on what that means in what is a non-custodial wallet.
