How do you sell a position on MOONX?

You never have to wait for the result. One button sells your shares at the going price, and the money comes back to your wallet, ready to use or withdraw.

By MOONX Team·Updated 27 October 2026·3 min read
Short answer

Tap Eject on your position. MOONX shows the price and the fee before you confirm, then sells your shares at the best price on the Polymarket order book once you confirm with your PIN, and the money goes back to your MOONX wallet. MOONX charges 1% of the sale value, and Polymarket's fee applies on most markets. On a thin or fast-moving market the price can be lower than shown, and a sale can fill partly, late or not at all.

How Eject works

A sale needs a buyer. On Polymarket, buyers post the prices they're willing to pay in an order book. Eject sells your shares right away to the best of those offers, called the bid.

The price you see on a market is the midpoint between the best bid and the best ask (the cheapest seller). So a sale right now usually fills a little below the price shown. The gap between the two is the spread. When the spread is wider than 10¢, Polymarket shows the last traded price instead.

MOONX shows the price and the fee before you confirm. You confirm with your PIN, like every trade.

Worked example

You hold 100 shares of Yes, bought at 20¢ for $20, in a politics market (Polymarket rate 0.04). The best bid is 40¢ and the best ask 42¢, so the price shown is the midpoint, 41¢. You tap Eject.

  • Sale: 100 × 0.40 = $40
  • MOONX fee: 1% of $40 = $0.40
  • Polymarket fee: 100 × 0.04 × 0.40 × 0.60 = $0.96

You receive 40 − 0.40 − 0.96 = $38.64. The fees you paid when you bought are already behind you.

When the price is not what you tapped

On a quiet market, there may not be enough buyers at the best bid for all your shares. Say buyers at 40¢ only want 60 of your 100 shares: the other 40 may sell lower, or not right away. On a fast market, the bid can move between your tap and the fill. A sale can fill partly, late or not at all. Shares that don't sell stay in your position.

You'll find more on this in what is slippage on Polymarket and what is liquidity on Polymarket.

Where the money goes

The money from the sale goes back to your MOONX wallet, in dollars. From there you can take another shot or withdraw it to an address on your Payout Wallets list, with no withdrawal fee. See how to withdraw from MOONX.

You can also just hold. When the market resolves, winning shares pay $1 each with no fee, and the money lands in your wallet, usually within minutes, sometimes a few hours.

When selling early makes sense, and when it doesn't, is covered in selling before the result.
