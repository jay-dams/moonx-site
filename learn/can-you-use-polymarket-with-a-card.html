Can you use Polymarket with a credit or debit card?

Trading on Polymarket needs a dollar token on a blockchain, not a card number. Whether you can start with a card depends on the app you use to reach it.

By MOONX Team·Updated 27 October 2026·3 min read
Short answer

Polymarket itself settles every trade in pUSD, a dollar token on Polygon, and its help center and developer docs describe deposits as crypto transfers from supported networks. MOONX, an app built on Polymarket, lets you add money by card, mobile wallet or bank transfer through its payment partners, Stripe and Swapped, via Calm Treasury. The partner's fee is shown before you pay, and the money arrives as USDC in your own wallet.

What Polymarket's own guides describe

Polymarket's help article on deposits and its developer documentation both describe the same method: sending crypto to a deposit address.

  • You get a deposit address for each type of network: Ethereum and other EVM chains, Solana, Bitcoin and Tron.
  • What you send is bridged and converted to pUSD, the dollar token used for all trading (what is pUSD?).
  • Each asset has a minimum. The help center gives $20 from Ethereum and $3 from Solana. Smaller deposits stay pending until the total reaches the minimum.
  • Polymarket charges no fee to deposit or withdraw. The service you use to move the money may charge its own.

These pages don't describe a card option. Polymarket's own app may show other ways to pay, so check it directly if you use it.

Paying by card on MOONX

MOONX is an app built on Polymarket. You can add money in three ways besides sending crypto:

MethodHow it works
CardPay by card through a payment partner
Mobile walletPay with the wallet on your phone
Bank transferSend money from your bank account

Payments go through Stripe and Swapped, via Calm Treasury. Their fee is shown before you pay, so you see what you will receive before you confirm. The money arrives as USDC on Polygon in your MOONX wallet, which only you control. When you trade, MOONX moves it to Polymarket as pUSD and pays the network fees.

Worked example

You add $50 by card and want to buy Yes at 40¢.

  1. The partner's screen shows its fee and the USDC you will get. Say it shows 49 USDC. You confirm.
  2. You buy Yes with $20. Before fees, that is 20 ÷ 0.40 = 50 shares.
  3. MOONX's fee is 1% of the trade: 1% of $20 is $0.20. Every fee is included in what you see before you confirm.
  4. You still have about 49 − 20 = 29 USDC in your wallet.

If Yes wins, the 50 shares pay $50. If No wins, they pay nothing.

Things to know before paying

  • The partner fee is separate from MOONX's 1% trading fee and Polymarket's taker fee (MOONX fees).
  • Payment partners may run their own identity checks before they accept your payment.
  • Withdrawals don't go back to the card. On MOONX, money leaves only to addresses on your Payout Wallets list, as USDC on Polygon (how to withdraw from MOONX).
  • Availability varies. Polymarket restricts some countries, and MOONX is not available everywhere (including the US, the UK, mainland China and Hong Kong; see the Terms). Check the rules where you live.
