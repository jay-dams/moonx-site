What are the fees on MOONX?

MOONX has one fee of its own, on the way in and on the way out. The rest of what you pay comes from Polymarket and from the service you use to add money.

By MOONX Team·Updated 27 October 2026·3 min read
Short answer

MOONX charges 1% of the trade value when you buy and 1% when you sell, shown before you confirm. There is no fee when a market resolves, no subscription, no account fee and no withdrawal fee, and MOONX pays the network (gas) fees. On most markets Polymarket's own taker fee applies as well, and the payment partners that sell you USDC by card show their own fee before you pay.

Every fee is included in what you see before you confirm. Because MOONX pays the gas, your wallet never needs to hold another token to trade, add money or withdraw. More on that in what are gas fees.

MOONX's Terms say there is currently no subscription. If one is ever introduced, existing users get at least 30 days' notice, and it will never be needed to reach your wallet or withdraw.

What others charge

  • Polymarket, on most markets: the trader whose order fills right away pays fee = shares × rate × price × (1 − price). As a share of the money you spend, that's rate × (1 − price). Rates go from 0 on geopolitics to 0.07 on crypto, with 0.05 on sports and 0.04 on politics.
  • Payment partners (Stripe and Swapped, through Calm Treasury), if you add money by card, mobile wallet or bank transfer. Their fee is shown before you pay.
  • The exchange or wallet you send USDC from, or withdraw to, may charge its own fee.
Worked example

You put $50 on Yes at 20¢ in a politics market (rate 0.04). You get 50 ÷ 0.20 = 250 shares.

  • MOONX fee: 1% of $50 = $0.50
  • Polymarket fee: 250 × 0.04 × 0.20 × 0.80 = $1.60

Fees to get in: $2.10.

If you hold and Yes wins: 250 × $1 = $250, with no fee at resolution. Profit after fees: 250 − 50 − 2.10 = $197.90. At 20¢, the market sees that as about a 1 in 5 chance.

If you sell at 40¢: 250 × 0.40 = $100. MOONX takes 1% = $1.00, Polymarket 250 × 0.04 × 0.40 × 0.60 = $2.40. You receive $96.60, a profit of 96.60 − 50 − 2.10 = $44.50.

If No wins: your shares pay nothing. You lose the $50 and the $2.10 in fees.

Where MOONX's fee goes

Part of it goes back to users: 25% of MOONX's fee on the trades of people who joined with your link, and 25% when someone trades a market from one of your trades. That comes out of MOONX's share. Nobody pays more because they were invited. Details in earn with MOONX.

For every Polymarket rate and the fee at each price, read Polymarket fees.
