Using MOONX

Your MOONX wallet: who holds the keys, and what you must keep

On MOONX, your money sits in a wallet that only you can move. That's a real protection, and it comes with one real responsibility. Here is how it works, in plain words.

By MOONX Team·Updated 27 October 2026·3 min read
Short answer

Your MOONX wallet is non-custodial: its keys are created on your device and MOONX never holds them, so MOONX can't move, freeze or spend your money. You keep one encrypted backup file and its password. Each device you use gets a PIN that approves every trade and money move. If you lose the backup file or its password, nobody can recover the wallet, MOONX included.

Non-custodial, in one sentence

MOONX is software that helps your device create and use a wallet. It never holds your money or your keys. In the words of MOONX's Terms, it has no ability to move, freeze or access a single dollar in your wallet.

Compare that with an exchange account: there, the company holds the money and you hold a login. If the company fails or freezes accounts, your balance is stuck. With a non-custodial wallet, the money is under your keys, on a public blockchain.

The three things you hold

WhatWhat it doesWho has it
Backup fileAn encrypted copy of your wallet keysYou only. MOONX doesn't keep a copy.

You log in with your email. Logging in shows your account. It's the PIN that lets money move, and the backup file with its password that lets you bring your wallet to a new device. You can also add passkeys to secure your account.

The one thing you must not lose

If you lose the backup file or its password, the money in that wallet is lost for everyone: one doesn't work without the other. MOONX can't restore it, by design. Store the file somewhere you won't delete it, such as a cloud drive and a USB stick, and the password in a password manager.

Using MOONX on another phone or computer

  1. Open MOONX on the new device and log in with your email.
  2. Upload your backup file.
  3. Enter its password. Your wallet, your money and your trades are all there.
  4. Choose a PIN for this device, the same one or a new one.

Where your money actually is

MOONX wallets live on the Polygon blockchain, the same network Polymarket runs on. Your wallet has up to three parts:

  • Main wallet: your dollars, in USDC. Deposits arrive here and withdrawals leave from here.
  • Trading wallet: the money in play on Polymarket, which settles in pUSD. MOONX moves money between the two when you trade.
  • Savings (optional): unused cash can earn interest through Aave, a third-party lending protocol, at a variable rate. You can ask for it back at any time. Withdrawals depend on Aave having money available, so they can be delayed, and lending protocols carry their own risks.

MOONX pays the network fees for all of this, so you never need to hold another token.

Withdrawals can only go to addresses on your Payout Wallets list, up to five, that you set yourself. It's a safeguard that runs in your wallet, not a service MOONX operates. Check each address carefully, because a withdrawal to a wrong address can't be reversed.

Common questions

Is MOONX custodial?

No. The wallet keys are generated on your device and never sent to MOONX. Only you can move your money.

What happens if I lose my phone?

Log in on another device with your email, upload your backup file and enter its password. Your wallet comes back with your money and your trades. Then choose a new PIN.

Can MOONX recover my wallet if I lose my backup file?

No. MOONX keeps no copy of your backup file or your password. Without both, nobody can restore the wallet.

Is there a seed phrase?

No. MOONX uses one encrypted backup file and a password instead of a list of words to write down.
