Using MOONX

Your first trade on MOONX, step by step

MOONX puts every Polymarket market in a social feed you can trade in a few taps, without handling crypto yourself. This is the whole path, from your email to your first sale, so nothing surprises you on the way.

By MOONX Team·Updated 27 October 2026·4 min read
Short answer

Sign up on MOONX with your email and explore the app. Then set up your wallet, which takes about two minutes: no seed phrase, one backup file and a password that only you keep, and a PIN of six to twelve digits. Add money with a card, or send USDC on Polygon. Then open a market, choose Yes or No and an amount, check the cost and confirm with your PIN. Sell anytime with Eject.

Before you start

You need an email address, a phone or a computer, and a payment card or some USDC. You don't need a crypto wallet, a seed phrase, or any token to pay network fees: MOONX handles that part.

You must be 18, or the age of majority where you live if that's higher. MOONX is not available everywhere, including the US and the UK. The full list is in the Terms.

1. Create your account

Go to moonx.fi and tap Create account. Enter your email and open the sign-in link MOONX sends you. There's no password to invent for logging in.

You can now explore the whole app: the feed, the markets, the prices.

2. Set up your wallet

To trade, you finish your onboarding and get your wallet. MOONX is invite-only for now: if the app asks for an access code, it shows you how to get one for free.

Your wallet is created on your own device, and only you can move the money in it. Setup takes about two minutes:

  1. Give your account a name.
  2. Your device creates your wallet.
  3. Save one backup file: it's an encrypted copy of your wallet.
  4. Choose a password for that file. Keep the file and the password somewhere safe: MOONX doesn't keep a copy, and losing either one means losing access for good.
  5. Choose a PIN of six to twelve digits for this device. It approves every trade and every money move.

Why this matters, and how to use the same wallet on another device, is explained in your MOONX wallet.

3. Add money

  • By card, mobile wallet or bank transfer: like any online purchase. The payment is handled by MOONX's payment partners, Stripe and Swapped through Calm Treasury, and any fee they charge is shown before you pay.
  • With USDC: if you already have some, send USDC on the Polygon network from another wallet or an exchange to your MOONX address. Sending on another network can lose the funds, so check the network twice.

Your money lands in your main wallet, in dollars (USDC).

4. Pick a market

Browse the feed of real trades from other MOONX traders, or search all of Polymarket: sports, crypto, politics, the news. Open a market and read its rules once: they say exactly what counts as Yes.

5. Choose Yes or No, and an amount

Each side shows its price, which is also the market's estimate of the odds. Enter your amount and MOONX shows how many shares you get and what they pay if you're right.

Worked example

Yes is at 20¢. You enter $10. You get 50 shares, which pay $50 if Yes wins: 5x your money, before fees. MOONX shows the full cost, fees included, before you confirm.

Confirm with your PIN. Your wallet signs the order and MOONX passes it to Polymarket, moving the money for you. You don't pay network fees.

6. Follow it, then sell or wait

Your position updates as the price moves. You have two ways out:

  • Sell anytime with Eject. One tap sells at the best price on the book. MOONX shows the price and the fee before you confirm. On a quiet market it can fill lower, or only partly. Useful to lock in a gain or limit a loss. See selling before the result.
  • Hold to the end. When the market resolves, winning shares pay $1 each, and the money lands in your MOONX wallet, usually within a few minutes, sometimes a few hours. You don't need to do anything.

7. Withdraw

First add the address you want to send to, such as an exchange account or another wallet that accepts USDC on Polygon, to your Payout Wallets list (up to five). Then withdraw whenever you want. There is no withdrawal fee, and network fees are on MOONX. A withdrawal to a wrong address can't be reversed, so check it twice.

What it costs

MOONX charges 1% of the trade value when you buy and when you sell. Polymarket's own fee applies on most markets, depending on the category and the price. Nothing is charged when a market resolves. Every fee is included in what you see before you confirm. All the numbers are in Polymarket fees.

Common questions

Do I need crypto to use MOONX?

No. You can add money with a card, a mobile wallet or a bank transfer. If you already hold USDC, you can also send it on the Polygon network.

How long does it take to start?

Signing up takes a minute and setting up the wallet about two more. Then you can add money and trade.

What is the minimum to trade?

Trades start at just over $1. Above 20¢ a share, a 5-share minimum can make it more, for example $2.50 at 50¢. See the minimum bet on Polymarket.

Can I use MOONX on my phone and my computer?

Yes, with the same wallet. Log in with your email on the other device, upload your backup file and enter its password, then choose a PIN for that device.
