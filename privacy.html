Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 September 2026 · MoonX · Hong Kong SAR

This Privacy Policy explains how MoonX collects, uses and protects your personal data when you use our website and app. It is written in plain language. If something is unclear, contact us.

⚠ Important · your keys and third parties

MoonX is non-custodial software. We never collect or hold your wallet keys, your backup, your backup password, your PIN or your funds.

When you trade, add funds or earn yield, you deal with independent third parties: Polymarket, our Payment Partners, the Aave lending protocol and the Polygon blockchain. They may collect and process your data independently of MoonX, under their own policies, and we have no control over what they do with it.

Everything your wallet does on a blockchain is public and permanent. This policy covers only the data that MoonX itself processes.

01

Who we are

MoonX is operated from Hong Kong and is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy.

Because we are based in Hong Kong, the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486) ("PDPO") is the primary law that applies to our processing of your personal data. If you are located in the European Union, the additional rights and protections in Section 12 (GDPR) also apply to you.

For data-protection enquiries, contact us at legal@moonx.fi.

02

Data we collect

Account data: your email address, used to create your account, verify it and sign you in, and the settings you choose.

Passkeys: if you add a passkey (your face, your fingerprint or a security key), we store only the public credential your device creates. Your biometrics never leave your device and never reach us.

Your Fund: the name you give your Fund, your profile, and the Shots you choose to show or share.

Wallet data: your wallet addresses and your Payout Wallets list, so the app knows which Fund is yours, and the public on-chain activity of those addresses (balances, trades, transfers, yield), which the app reads to show you your positions and your ledger.

Referral data: if you joined through someone's link, which link it was and when you joined. If people join through yours, the trade fees their trades generate, so that your referral earnings can be calculated and paid.

Alerts: if you turn on alerts on Telegram, the Telegram account or chat identifier needed to send them.

Usage and device data: information about how you use MoonX, such as screens viewed, features used, timestamps, device and browser type, and your IP address.

Communications: if you contact us, the messages and anything you attach to them.

Identity information: we do not generally collect it. If we are required by law or by a competent authority to verify your identity, we will collect only what that requires and tell you at the time.

We never collect your wallet keys, your encrypted backup, your backup password or your PIN. They are created on your device and stay there. The app is built so that they never reach us, which is also why we cannot restore a wallet for you.

03

How we use your data

We use your data to provide and improve MoonX: to operate your account and sign you in, to show you your Fund, your positions and your ledger, to pass the orders your wallet signs to Polymarket, to calculate and pay referral earnings, to send alerts you asked for, and to answer you when you write to us.

We use usage and analytics data to understand how MoonX is used and to make it better. We do not sell your personal data.

We use your email address to send service messages and security alerts, and, with your consent, product updates. You can opt out of product updates at any time.

04

What other people can see

Your public profile: your Fund name, and Shots worth showing, can appear on the Feed, on Legends, on your Fund's profile and on cards you share. Your Fund name is not your legal name unless you make it one.

The person who referred you: if you joined through someone's link, they see your Fund name, a masked version of your email address (for example to•••ks@gmail.com) and the date you joined, and later the referral earnings your trades generate for them. The masking happens on our side, and they never see your full address.

Anyone on the blockchain: see Section 6.

05

Data sharing

We share your data only where necessary to operate the service.

Our processors: technology partners that run parts of MoonX on our behalf, including hosting and database, email delivery and analytics (Google LLC, via Google Analytics). They may use your data only to perform services for us, under an appropriate data-processing agreement, and are prohibited from using it for their own purposes, including marketing to you. A full, current list is available on request at legal@moonx.fi.

Independent third parties: the following are not our processors. Your data relationship with them is governed by their own policies, not this one:

  • Polymarket receives the orders your wallet signs, and your wallet address.
  • Payment Partners (currently Stripe and Swapped, provided through Calm Treasury, calmtreasury.xyz) handle card, mobile-wallet and bank purchases of USDC. They collect your payment details, and may run their own identity checks, directly; we never receive your card details.
  • Aave, if you use the Yield Wallet, and the Polygon network, which record your wallet's transactions publicly.
  • Telegram, if you turn on alerts there.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data. We may share data with law-enforcement or regulatory authorities where required by law, and with a successor entity in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, under this policy.

06

Wallet and on-chain data

Your wallet is self-custodial and is created on your own device. We never collect or store your wallet keys or funds, and we have no ability to access or move them.

Every trade, payout, gift, deposit and withdrawal is a transaction on a public blockchain. Anyone who knows your wallet address can read all of it, permanently, and nobody, including us, can edit, delete or reverse it. Linking your Fund name to your wallet address, for example on Legends or a shared card, can make that activity easier to attribute to you.

07

Data retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active, and for 12 months after closure. Referral and fee records are kept for as long as needed to pay earnings and meet our accounting obligations. Communications are retained for 24 months. Analytics data is retained in anonymised form for 24 months. Retention periods may be extended where required by applicable law.

Closing your MoonX account does not touch your wallet: the funds and the keys remain yours, and the blockchain keeps its record, which is not ours to remove.

08

Your rights

Under the PDPO, you have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, to request correction of inaccurate data, and to ask how your data is used. Depending on your location and applicable law, you may also have rights to delete, restrict, port or object to certain processing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at legal@moonx.fi. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law, and in any event within 30 days.

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data-protection authority. In Hong Kong, this is the Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data (PCPD), reachable via pcpd.org.hk. EU residents may also contact their local supervisory authority (see Section 12).

MoonX does not make solely automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects concerning you.

09

Security

We implement technical and organisational measures designed to reduce the risk of unauthorised access to, loss of, or disclosure of your personal data. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your wallet keys are generated and held on your own device and are designed never to be transmitted to or stored by MoonX. This reflects the architectural design of the software, not a guarantee against every possible failure; securing your device, your backup, your backup password and your PIN is your responsibility. We will never ask you for them.

In the event of a data breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify you and the relevant authority within 72 hours of becoming aware, where required by applicable law.

10

Cookies, storage and analytics

MoonX uses strictly necessary cookies and browser storage to operate the service, including keeping you signed in, security tokens, remembering where you are in setup, and settings such as your MoonX Radio station and volume. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

We use Google Analytics to collect anonymised data about how MoonX is used, including pages viewed, session duration, approximate (country-level) location, and device and browser type. IP addresses are anonymised before processing. Where the law requires your consent for analytics, we ask for it first and load nothing until you agree.

11

Children and changes to this policy

Children: MoonX is not for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data about a minor, and if we learn that we have, we delete it.

Changes: we may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the effective date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, by email. For material changes, such as new data uses or new third-party sharing, we will endeavour to notify you by email at least 14 days before the change takes effect, where reasonably practicable. Continued use of MoonX after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

12

European Union users · GDPR

Applicability: although MoonX is operated from Hong Kong, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies to our processing of personal data of individuals located in the European Union, in accordance with Article 3 GDPR. If you are located in the EU, the rights and protections set out in this section apply to you.

EU Representative: in accordance with Article 27 GDPR, MoonX has appointed Mr. Pedro Sanchez as EU Representative, located in the European Union. EU users may contact our EU Representative at legal@moonx.fi, marked for his attention.

Data transfers from the EU to Hong Kong: Hong Kong is not recognised by the European Commission as providing an adequate level of data protection. Where we transfer personal data from the EU to Hong Kong or to other countries without an adequacy decision, we do so on the basis of Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) adopted by the European Commission, or another valid transfer mechanism under Chapter V GDPR. You may request a copy of the applicable transfer mechanism at legal@moonx.fi.

Legal bases for processing: we process EU users' personal data on the following bases under Article 6 GDPR: contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b)) for operating your account, your Fund and the referral programme; legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) for any verification required by law; legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) for security, fraud prevention and showing your public profile; and consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) for analytics where required and for optional communications. You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior processing.

Your GDPR rights: as an EU resident you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your personal data, and to object to processing based on legitimate interests. To exercise these rights, contact legal@moonx.fi. We will respond within 30 days. Erasure cannot reach data recorded on a public blockchain, which no one can delete.

Right to complain: you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local EU supervisory authority. A full list of EU data-protection authorities is available at edpb.europa.eu.

Contact

Privacy questions or data requests? Write to legal@moonx.fi. EU Representative: Mr. Pedro Sanchez, at the same address.
