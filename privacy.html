PRIVACY

your keys, and almost nothing else.

The short version: we hold an email, the name of your fund and the region you are in. We do not hold your money, your key, your backup password or your PIN, and we could not hand them over if anyone asked. This website sets no cookies unless you say yes to one question, and the answer is remembered on your machine, not in a cookie.

LAST UPDATED 11 September 2026

who this is about.

MOONX is the one deciding what happens to the data described here. Write to hello@moonx.fi about any of it, including to ask us to delete it.

what we collect.

Your email

Given at signup, and only if you give it: the game never asks for it. It is used to tell you when the door opens, and for the few messages an account needs once it does. It is stored by Supabase, our database host, on servers in the European Union.

The handle on a ride

The name you type at the end of a ride, sent with your score so the board can show it. It is attached to the ride, not to you: nothing links two rides unless you also left an email.

The name of your fund

You choose it. It appears on your card and on the board if you land a shot worth showing. It is not your legal name unless you make it one.

Your region

Read at signup from your connection, to tell you what is available where you are. We keep the answer, not a history of where you have been.

An invitation code

If you arrived through somebody's link, we keep which link, so their rides and their share are counted.

What you write to us

Support messages and anything you attach to them, kept while the question is open and for a while after in case it comes back.

Your wallet address

Public by nature. We hold it so the app knows which fund is yours. It is not a secret and it never was.

what we never collect.

Your private key, your backup password, your PIN, and any file that could rebuild them. These are made on your device and stay there. This is not a policy we could relax later: the app is built so that they never reach us, which is also why we cannot restore an account for you.

We do not collect your legal name, your date of birth, a document, a selfie, a phone number, your contacts, your location beyond the region check, or anything from another app on your device.

what this website stores on your machine.

Nothing runs until you say so. On your first visit we ask one question: may we count visits. Say no and nothing loads, nothing is sent, and we do not ask again. Say yes and the pages load Google Analytics, which sets its own cookies to count you once rather than twice.

We ask Google for the plainest version of that: your IP address is truncated before it is stored, advertising storage and personalisation stay switched off, and we never send them your email, your handle or anything you typed. We look at how many people came and which pages they opened. Nothing else.

Your answer is kept in browser storage under moonx.consent, not in a cookie: setting a cookie to remember that you refuse cookies would be a joke. Clear your browser storage and we will ask again. No advertising pixels, no third-party trackers, no fingerprinting, and apart from Google Analytics if you allowed it, the pages load fonts, images and code from this domain and from nowhere else.

Moon Rodeo saves a few values on your own device, in browser storage, so the game remembers you between rides. They never leave the machine and there is no account behind them:

  • sr.set your settings: lyrics, title card, screen shake, vibration.
  • sr.meta the input delay the game learned from your taps.
  • sr.best your best multiple per song.
  • sr.daily whether today's ride has been used.
  • sr.handle the name you ride under, if you set one.
  • sr.board the rides you finished on this device, so the board is not empty before the season opens.
  • moonx.me the last handle you used, and your email if you left one.
  • moonx.consent your answer to the analytics question.

Clearing your browser data clears all of it, and the game starts fresh.

what is public for ever.

Every shot, every payout and every withdrawal is a transaction on a public blockchain. Anyone who knows your wallet address can read all of it, permanently, and nobody, including us, can delete or amend it. This is the trade a non-custodial market makes: no company between you and your money, and no company able to erase the record either.

who else touches it.

  • Polymarket, the venue where the markets live, receives the orders your wallet signs.
  • The card provider used to buy USDC runs its own checks and holds whatever it needs for them. That relationship is between you and them, and its rules are theirs.
  • Supabase, our database host, stores the email and the handle described above, on servers in the European Union.
  • Google Analytics, and only if you allowed it, receives a truncated IP address and the page you opened. Never your email, never your handle, never anything you typed.
  • Our email provider handles the sending of sign-in links and receipts.

Nobody buys this data from us, because we do not sell it, and we do not trade it for services either.

how long we keep it.

Your email and fund name for as long as the account is open, and for a short period after you close it so a duplicate signup or a dispute can be resolved. Support messages for two years. The region answer until it changes. On-chain records for ever, because they are not ours to keep or to remove.

what you can ask for.

A copy of what we hold, a correction, a deletion, a machine-readable export, or a stop to a particular use. Write to hello@moonx.fi and we will answer within thirty days. Deleting your MOONX account does not touch your wallet: the funds and the key remain yours, and the chain keeps its record. If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority.

children.

MOONX is not for anyone under eighteen. We do not knowingly hold anything about a minor, and if we learn we do, we delete it.

when this changes.

The date at the top moves and the account holders get an email if the change matters. We do not make quiet edits to this page.
