TERMS OF SERVICE

what we do, and what we cannot do.

Plain rules for a product where the money is never ours. Using MOONX means agreeing to them. If a line here does not suit you, do not open a fund.

LAST UPDATED 11 September 2026

what MOONX is.

MOONX is software. It shows you the prediction markets on Polymarket, helps your device make and keep a wallet, and passes the orders your wallet signs to that venue. It pays the network fees so you do not have to.

MOONX is not a broker, a bank, an exchange, a custodian, an investment adviser or a bookmaker. We never take the other side of your position and we make nothing from you losing. Nothing on this site or in the app is financial advice, and no number anywhere on it is a promise of a return.

who can use it.

You must be at least eighteen, and old enough where you live if that is more. You must not be in a country where these markets are restricted, or on a sanctions list. It is on you to know where you stand. One account per person.

your wallet is yours, with everything that means.

The wallet is created on your device. The private key, the encrypted backup file, the password to that file and your PIN are yours alone. We do not hold copies, we cannot recover them, and we cannot move, freeze or unfreeze a single dollar in that wallet.

So: if you lose the backup file or its password, the funds are lost, permanently, to everyone including us. Keeping them safe is your responsibility and it is the only one this product really asks of you. We will never ask you for any of them, by email, in chat, or in the app. Anyone who does is trying to rob you.

taking a shot.

An order goes to Polymarket and settles under Polymarket's rules on Polymarket's timing. The price you get is the price on the book when it fills, which on a thin market can differ from the number you tapped. A market resolves on its published source, and if that source is disputed, the resolution is handled by the venue, not by us. Blockchains and third-party services fail sometimes: an order can be delayed or rejected and we do not guarantee that any particular one goes through.

fees.

A fee on each shot, shown before you confirm it, on the way in and the way out. No subscription, no account fee, no withdrawal fee. Network fees are on us. If the fee changes, it changes for shots taken after the change, and you see the new number before you confirm.

invitations.

Bring somebody in with your link and you earn a share of our fee, up to 0.5%, when they take or sell a shot. It comes out of our side and never out of theirs. Invented accounts, bought traffic and rings of accounts inviting each other are not invitations: we can withhold the share and close the accounts.

moon rodeo.

The game on this website is free and needs no account: one song, one life, and as many rides as you like. Put a handle on the board if you want your ride to stand. Nothing is won and nothing is paid: the board is the whole prize, for now.

  • Nothing is minted and nothing is sold. The card at the end of a ride is an image, not an asset, not a token, and it carries no value or ownership.
  • One ride a day, plus one for every cowboy who actually rides on your link.
  • Automation, modified clients, several accounts for one person, or any other way of playing that is not a thumb on a screen voids the scores and the stake that came from them.
  • Scores are what our server records. A game that ends because a connection dropped is a game that ended.

We do not promise it is available at all times, free of bugs, or fit for a purpose you have in mind, and we do not promise the same for Polymarket, for Polygon, or for any other network or provider in the chain. As far as the law allows, we are not liable for losses from a market moving against you, from a key or a password you lost, from a chain or a venue failing, from a third-party service breaking, or from anyone getting hold of your device. risk, said once and plainly.

Prediction markets can lose you every dollar you put on a shot. Long shots are cheap because they usually do not land. Put in what you can lose without it changing anything about your life, and never what you cannot.

ending it.

Close your account whenever you like. We can suspend or close an account that breaks these terms, or stop serving a region we are told to stop serving. In every one of those cases your wallet stays yours: the key is on your device, the funds and the positions remain reachable with your backup, and no closure by us can touch them.

The date at the top moves, and account holders get an email if the change matters. Continuing to use MOONX after that is how you accept the new version. If a court finds one clause unenforceable, the rest still stands.
