getting started.

What is MOONX?

A way to take a position on a real prediction market from your phone, in one tap, without handing your money to anybody. You pick a market, you put dollars on the outcome you believe, and you hold a share that you can sell back at any time until it settles. The markets are on Polymarket. The money sits in a wallet only you can open.

What do I need to sign up?

An email and two minutes. You give an email, name your fund, save one encrypted backup file with a password only you hold, and set a PIN of six to twelve digits. MOONX then opens the bridge to Polymarket and you can take a shot. Five steps, no documents, no broker.

Do I have to send an ID?

No. There is no identity check, no document to upload, no approval to wait for. That is not a shortcut, it is what non-custodial means: the wallet is made on your device and the keys stay there, so we never hold your money and we have no reason to hold your papers either.

We do check which country you are connecting from at signup, because prediction markets are regulated differently from one place to the next. If we cannot serve you, we say so plainly instead of taking your money first.

How much do I need to start?

It depends on the market. Each one carries its own floor, set by Polymarket, and the smallest of them start at one dollar. There is no account minimum on our side and nothing to fund before you look around.

What you put on a shot is your call and we will never suggest a number. The only thing worth knowing is the one further down this page: you can lose all of it.

Do I need crypto to start?

No. Buy USDC with a card in the app, the way you would buy anything else. If you already hold USDC, send it from any wallet or exchange. And you never pay gas: MOONX covers the network fees.

how the market works.

Is this gambling?

No. You are buying a share in a real prediction market, and you can sell it back at any time before it settles. A ticket at 4¢ is worth 4¢ the second after you buy it, not zero. What you pay is the market price, set by everyone trading it, not odds set by a house. MOONX is not the counterparty and does not profit from you losing.

link to this answer

Where do the prices come from?

From the order book on Polymarket, where everybody trading that market meets. A price of 5¢ is the crowd saying it happens about one time in twenty. Nobody at MOONX sets it, moves it or sees your order before the venue does. Any price printed on this site is a reading with the date it was taken, never an example we made up.

Why do the payouts look so big?

Because a long shot is cheap. A ticket at 5¢ pays a dollar if it lands, which is 20x. That also means the market thinks it lands one time in twenty. Big multiples and low odds are the same fact seen from two sides.

What happens when a market settles?

The market closes on a fact, not on an opinion: an election is called, a number is published, a date passes. Shares on the outcome that happened are worth one dollar each, the others are worth nothing, and the payout lands back in your wallet. You do not have to be holding at the end: you can sell a position at the going price at any point before that.

What can I actually take a position on?

Everything Polymarket lists, and that is the point: an election, a rate decision, whether a film takes an Oscar, whether a coin holds a level by a date, whether a launch happens this quarter. Thousands of markets, opening and closing all the time.

You do not need to know a market to find one: the board is sorted by what is moving, and every market carries its own question in plain words, with its resolution date next to it.

link to this answer

Who decides the outcome, and what if it is contested?

Not us. A market resolves on Polymarket, on the rules written into that market before anyone took a position, and a disputed resolution goes through Polymarket's own process. We follow it. MOONX has no say in whether you won, which is exactly what you want from the app that holds the board.

Read the resolution rules before you take a position, not after. They are on every market, and they are the only thing that decides.

When do I get paid?

At resolution. Once the market settles, the winning shares pay one dollar each and the money lands in your wallet: a few minutes, sometimes a few hours. It is on-chain, so you can watch it happen rather than take our word for it.

You do not have to wait for resolution to take money off the table. A share can be sold back at the going price at any time before the market closes.

Can I sell before it settles?

Yes, that is the point of a market. If a shot you took at 6¢ trades at 30¢ a week later, you can take that now instead of waiting to find out. The price you get is the price on the book at that moment, which on a thin market can be worse than the number on the screen.

your money and your keys.

Where does my money actually go?

Into your own wallet, on Polygon, in USDC. MOONX never holds it. When you take a shot, the order goes to Polymarket, the venue where the market lives. When it settles, the payout comes back to the same wallet.

What is a non-custodial wallet, in plain terms?

A wallet only you can move money out of. It is made on your device when you name your fund. You save one encrypted backup file and set a password only you hold; together they are the only way back in, and you can export the key and walk away with your funds at any moment. No approval from us, ever.

link to this answer

What if I lose the backup file or the password?

Then the wallet is gone, and so is what is in it. This is the hard edge of the promise above: a company that can restore your account for you is a company that can empty it. We hold neither the file nor the password, so there is nothing for us to send you and nothing for anyone to take from us. Save the file in two places on the day you make it. Treat the password like the only key to a house.

link to this answer

What is the PIN for, and is it the password?

Two different things. The password unlocks the encrypted backup file, and is what brings your wallet back on a new device. The PIN, six to twelve digits, signs transactions on the device you are already using, so a phone left on a table is not a fund left open. Losing the PIN is an inconvenience, because the backup restores you. Losing the password is not.

Can MOONX freeze my funds or close a position for me?

No. We cannot move your money, close a shot, or stop a withdrawal, because the key that signs those actions is on your device and not on our servers. We can stop serving the app to a region or an account, and that is the full extent of it: you would still hold every dollar and every position, and could take them out with the backup file.

link to this answer

What happens to my money if MOONX disappears?

Nothing. Your funds are not with us and never were: the key that moves them was made on your device and lives on your device. If this company folded tomorrow, your backup file and your password would still open that wallet, and your positions would still settle on Polymarket without us.

That is the whole reason the setup is built this way, and it is also why we cannot get your money back if you lose the backup and the password. The two go together: there is no version where we can rescue you and cannot also freeze you.

How do I take my money out?

Withdraw to any wallet or exchange address that takes USDC on Polygon, from inside the app, with your PIN. There is no approval queue and no minimum holding time. If you would rather leave entirely, export the private key from the backup file and open the wallet in any wallet app you like: at that point MOONX is not involved at all.

what it costs.

What does it cost?

A small fee on each shot, shown to you before you confirm it, taken on the way in and on the way out of a position. There is no monthly charge, no account fee, no charge for withdrawing, and no charge for holding a position however long it runs.

Do I pay gas?

No. Every transaction on Polygon costs network fees, and MOONX pays them for you, including the one that moves your money out. You will never be stopped by a wallet that holds dollars but not the token that pays for the transfer.

How does MOONX make money?

A small fee on each shot, disclosed before you take it. If you bring a cowboy in, you earn up to 0.5% each time they take or sell a shot, taken from our side, not theirs.

What can I lose?

Exactly what you put on a shot, and nothing more. Ten dollars on a ticket means ten dollars at risk. There is no leverage, no margin call, no debt.
